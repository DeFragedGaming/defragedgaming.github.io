SIMULATED DATA
What happened

Several accounts received failed logins from one source over a short window. A successful login for one of those accounts came from a separate, unrecognized device.

What you need to decide

Determine whether the failures share a pattern, identify which account needs review, then apply scoped containment and account protection.

Safety and scope

This is a fictional, client-side SOC exercise. Commands only query the built-in sample data or simulate defensive actions; nothing is sent over the network.
