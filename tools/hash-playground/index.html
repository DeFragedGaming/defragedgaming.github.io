Teaching notes
  • Hashing: one‑way, fixed‑length output. Great for integrity checks and password storage (with salts and slow KDFs).
  • Encoding: reversible representation. Use it for transport and formatting, not security.
  • Show how the same input looks across SHA‑256 vs SHA‑1 vs MD5, and why modern suites prefer SHA‑2+.
  • Pair this lab with logs or packet captures in other CyberTrace labs to show where hashes and encodings appear in the wild.
